### Summary of the paper (as this reviewer understands it)

This is an IEEE conference-format empirical study (IEEEtran, `conference` mode, 11 pages). It is a pre-registered measurement of structurally-valid-but-semantically-invalid (SV-SI) inputs at a web application's input boundary, comparing seven generation conditions. It also evaluates five candidate semantic validation layers. The draft covers Sections I–XI, with RQ6, the Abstract and the Conclusion still pending.

### Strengths

- The prose is mostly concise and precise. The red-flag vocabulary is almost entirely absent: none of "significantly", "novel", "robust", "state-of-the-art", "seamless", "leverage" or "delve" occurs in `paper.tex` or `tables/*.tex`.
- Key terms are defined operationally in Sec. III, including SV-SI and conditional versus unconditional rate.
- Tables are generated by scripts and carry self-contained captions with CIs. Deviations and post hoc tests are labelled in the text.

### Guidance for the Abstract and Conclusion (style side)

- **Abstract structure** (about 200–250 words, IEEE): problem → gap → approach → key results → significance.
  - No citations.
  - Define LLM and SV-SI at first use.
  - No `\pending` numbers except RQ6, if E4 is still missing at submission.
  - Replace the current abstract's last sentence ("All quantitative results are marked \pending{experiment}"). It is now false for RQ1–RQ5.
  - Replace "We further specify an evaluation ..." with the measured outcome.
- **Conclusion.** Delete the "\pending{Conclusion ...}" paragraph. Rewrite ¶1: "This paper supplies what is needed to measure ... a specification for evaluating ..." describes a protocol paper, and the study has now been executed. Use past tense, state the findings with their numbers and CIs, and state the negative results (H3a–c, H5) as findings, not failures.
- **Keep the future-work paragraph.** Add: completing the human-written conditions and judge–human κ, and a per-prompt-family analysis.
- **Do not use in either:** "significant(ly)" (unless tied to a named test), "novel", "first", "robust", "deployable" for layers that missed the target, or "human" for conditions A/G without qualification.
