# Venue shortlist: where this paper can be published at no cost

Date: 2026-10-17 · Status: for the authors to choose from; run `/rs-venue <name>` after choosing.

Two facts frame every option below. First, no reputable venue accepts a paper without peer review
and the revisions it asks for; a journal that promises acceptance without changes is a predatory
one, and publishing there would harm the authors more than not publishing. Second, "free" here means
the subscription (non open access) route of a hybrid journal, or a diamond open access journal. On the
subscription route the article sits behind a paywall, but the authors keep the right to post the
accepted manuscript on arXiv, and the artefacts are already public on GitHub.

Fit for this paper: empirical software engineering, web application security, LLM inputs, a
pre-registered experiment with a public artefact. The paper is 10 to 11 IEEE pages with 48 references.

## Recommended order

| # | Venue | Cost | Fit | Selectivity | Why this position |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **e-Informatica Software Engineering Journal** (Wroclaw Univ. of Science and Technology) | Diamond OA, no fee, CC BY 4.0 | Strong: scope is empirical software engineering and AI/ML in SE; no page limit | Moderate; Scopus and Web of Science indexed, CiteScore 3.5 to 4.5, impact factor 1.2 to 2.0 depending on year | Best balance of free, indexed and realistic. Continuous publishing, so no issue delay |
| 2 | **Software Quality Journal** (Springer) | Hybrid; subscription route has no fee | Good: validation, testing and quality assurance; a 2026 special issue "Software Quality in an AI-Driven World" was announced | Moderate | Validation-layer evaluation and the detection/overhead trade-off are squarely in scope |
| 3 | **Software: Practice and Experience** (Wiley) | Hybrid; subscription route has no fee | Good: practice-oriented empirical work with released code | Moderate | Reviewers value the artefact and the measured overhead |
| 4 | **Journal of Systems and Software** or **Information and Software Technology** (Elsevier) | Hybrid; subscription route has no fee (OA option about USD 3,700, not needed) | Strong for both | High (roughly one in five) | Well-regarded empirical SE journals; expect a major-revision round |
| 5 | **IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering** | No mandatory fee up to 10 printed pages; USD 220 per page beyond 10; USD 110 per page voluntary; OA optional at USD 2,800 | Strong | Very high | The manuscript is already in IEEEtran journal mode. The 10-page threshold matters: the journal build is about 11 pages, so trim one page or accept one overlength page charge |
| 6 | **IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing** | Same IEEE model; regular length 12 pages, mandatory overlength above that | Good for the security framing | Very high | Fits if the authors position the paper as a security measurement rather than SE |
| 7 | **Computers & Security** (Elsevier) | Hybrid; subscription route has no fee (OA option USD 3,190) | Moderate: input validation is in scope, but the paper is more SE than security | High | Alternative to 6 |

Not recommended for a no-cost submission: IEEE Access (APC about USD 2,000), PeerJ Computer Science
(APC), ACM TOSEM and other ACM journals (ACM moved to full open access with APCs; a 100 per cent
waiver applies only to countries in ACM's EIFL/Research4Life Group A list and Pakistan's status could not
be confirmed, so check the ACM geographic waiver page before relying on it), and any conference, because
registration fees apply even when there is no publication charge.

## Free, immediate, not peer-reviewed

Post the accepted or submitted manuscript on **arXiv (cs.SE, cross-list cs.CR)**. It costs nothing,
gives a citable timestamp for the novelty claim, and every journal above permits preprints. Do this
on the day of submission, not before the journal is chosen, because a few journals want to be told.

## What each submission will need from the authors

- Second author's affiliation and email, and a contribution statement (TH-13).
- ORCIDs for both authors (free to create).
- A cover letter naming the venue and stating that the work is not under review elsewhere.
- The AI-use disclosure sentence stays in the acknowledgment; every venue above requires disclosure.
- Journals 1 to 4 want their own LaTeX or Word template, not IEEEtran. Convert after acceptance where
  the journal allows a free-format first submission (Springer, Elsevier and Wiley do).
